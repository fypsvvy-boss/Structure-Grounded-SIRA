== Reading (written by hand, 2026-10-14) ==
- Part 1 passes: 6,044 documents in both indexes; 46/46 tokens found; each stored expansion field is
  exactly the record's expansion_query(); the three unparseable replies have an empty expansion.
- Part 2 is mixed, which is what plain field summing at boost 1.0 predicts. Login failures: the brute-force
  family (T1110, T1110.003, T1110.004) moves into the top 5 through "login"/"failure" in their expansion.
  Scheduled task: T1053.005 stays first. Use-after-free and phishing: the right entry drops one or two places
  because a CVE or a CWE with a matching expansion word overtakes it. Path traversal: neither index finds a
  CWE-22-style entry in the top 5, and in the enriched one "folder" in short expansion fields decides the order.
- Why: the expansion field is a handful of words, and BM25 normalises by field length, so one word matched
  there scores like several in a long description. 05_MODULE3_STATE.md already names the field boosts
  (retrieval.fields) as the knob; this is the first sight of it on the real index. Tuning belongs to Module 3,
  on the dev queries, not on these five.
- Found by this test and fixed before the final build: 18 accepted structural ids written by the model as
  "ATT&CK T1499" / "mitre attack t1499" / "capec 112" had that text indexed, putting "mitre" and "attack" into
  12 expansion fields. expansion_query() now adds the normalised id only (T1499.004 left query 5's top 5).
